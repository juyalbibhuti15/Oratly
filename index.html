FAQ

Common questions.

Where can I download Oratly?+

Oratly is available on the App Store. Android version currently in testing.

Who is Oratly for?+

Oratly is for professionals who want to improve how they speak in meetings, presentations, interviews, and other high-pressure work situations.

Why not just use my phone camera?+

A phone can record you, but it does not provide structured workplace prompts or communication feedback. Oratly gives you a repeatable practice flow before the real moment.

Do I need to be good at public speaking already?+

No. Oratly is meant for people who want to build skill and confidence through practice — not just those who are already polished.

Is practice private?+

Yes. Oratly is designed for private practice so you can rehearse without coworkers watching. Your recording is used to generate your practice feedback.

What kind of practice does Oratly include?+

Real workplace scenarios, Think on Your Feet prompts, and short speaking exercises designed to help you practice before high-pressure conversations.

What kind of feedback does Oratly provide?+

Oratly reviews Directness, Structure, and Conciseness and shows what worked, what could improve, and an alternative way to start your response.
